# Office Politics Phase 4

Phase 4 replaces the persistent six-coworker bonus model with a political
office that changes on every floor.

## Player-Facing Changes

- Seven fixed six-person rosters now provide forty-two stable coworker
  identities. Karen, Dave, Bob, Janet, Raj, and Priya remain the Intern team.
- Relationship is floor-local from 0 to 10. Standing separately tracks whether
  each coworker supports the player or Chad.
- The Office Politics board shows stakeholders, Relationship, standing,
  personal agenda, queued scenes, Chad conversations, and predicted ballots.
- Every coworker owns hostile, ordinary, trusted, and loyal stories. Workday
  politics resolves at most one queued Relationship scene per day.
- Chad talks with one coworker daily. Those conversations visibly move
  standing and can create a Night Opening.
- Night Outings are now invitations with Build Rapport, Ask for Support, and
  Undermine Chad approaches.
- Day 1 Intern and Day 3 later-floor role votes award project-specific
  benefits. Loyalty guarantees the player ballot.
- Loyal former coworkers enter Alumni, unlock archetype perks, and can send
  Night messages and gifts.
- Delegation uses floor-local campaigns. Schmoozing uses a persistent Favor
  hand rather than permanent Contacts.
- Social cards and rewards now use Relationship, standing, invitations,
  campaigns, Alumni, and Favors instead of Coworker Bonus Activations.

## Compatibility

Active-run schema is 7. Incompatible active runs are cleared with a visible
notice while settings and tutorial metadata remain.

The Guided Intern overhaul remains Phase 6. The temporary orientation now
describes the Office Politics board and invitation model without teaching the
removed bonus system.
