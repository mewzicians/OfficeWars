# OfficeWars Task Keywords, Celestial Direction, And Night Planner

Release date: 2026-08-11

## Player-Facing Changes

- Rules-loaded task-card terms now use the shared high-contrast keyword
  treatment and expose concise definitions through hover, focus, and tap.
- Sunset and sunrise move both celestial bodies from screen-right/east toward
  screen-left/west without crossing.
- Night opens through a consolidated iPad-style Planner with Home Shop,
  Outings, Relationships, and conditional Deal Desk tabs.
- The Planner keeps Stress, Lights Out, Finalize Night, and Hide Planner
  available while changing tabs.
- The room remains fully usable through the Planner iPad, Home Shop computer,
  Social door, Deal Desk laptop, Lights Out switch, and End Day bed.
- Finalizing from the Planner or bed remembers that choice for the next Night.
- The first-play orientation now uses ten callouts. Its single Night lesson
  opens the Planner, explains coworker bonus timing, and introduces the room as
  an optional interface.

## Compatibility

- Meta schema 4 migrates completed and in-progress tutorial state from the
  former thirteen-step sequence into the ten-step sequence.
- Existing active-run saves remain compatible.
- No card, trait, economy, stress, or project balance value changed.
