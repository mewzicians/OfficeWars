# OfficeWars Superseded Design Archive

Last updated: 2026-09-21

This document is an index to historical OfficeWars material. Nothing listed
here is active or approved for implementation unless it was later restored to
`BALANCE_LEDGER.md`.

- `archive/BALANCE_LEDGER_FULL_2026-08-03.md` preserves the complete ledger
  before the clarity cleanup. It contains the abandoned 50-card roster,

  handoff.

  pre-rewrite music guide, including its chronological Casino candidate log,
  detailed measurements, and rule-oriented research notes.
- `archive/MUSIC_GUIDE_OFFICEWARS_WORKING_2026-08-13.md` preserves the
  intermediate cohesive guide before general music practice, research, and
  OfficeWars-specific direction were separated into their current files.
- `archive/officewars-home-handoff-2026-07-30/` preserves the older portable
  planning snapshot and its source copies.

The full archive files remain only in the source workspace and are
intentionally omitted from portable rollout packages.

Use these only when the reason behind an old decision matters. Do not use them
to resume current planning.

## Completed Planning Gates

- Statements dated before 2026-08-06 that describe the real HTML as the old
  task, random-rarity, or stackable Resume implementation are superseded. The
  active HTML now contains the rollout and readability candidate. The current
  full verification passes readability and automated implementation checks;
  structural tuning and explicitly unverified rows remain separate work.
- The August 3-4 roster-restart phase, including notes that Sales or Operations
  still needed cards, is complete. All 50 ordinary family cards are now
  individually approved.
- The standalone Common micro-effect budget gate is superseded by the approved
  individual Common cards. Their exact values are now reviewed with the full
  roster rather than waiting on a separate global budget.
- Brand Strategy's unlock was moved from a Legendary Design task to the Special
  card `Rebrand Initiative`.
- The broad prohibition on persistent ordinary-card effects was narrowed after
  approving the card-local Commit and Iteration Pass progression exceptions.

## Retired Distribution Copies

- The portable rollout package `officewars-rollout-handoff-2026-07-31/` was
  retired on 2026-08-26 and moved byte-intact to
  `../offie-war-quarantine-2026-08-24/retired-rollout-mirror/`. Of its 174
  files, 169 were byte-identical to the root, 0 differed, and 5 were
  mirror-only. `VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md` duplicated
  `verification/VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md` exactly, and `HANDOFF.md` was a
  remapped copy of `docs/HANDOFF.md` that had drifted by one line because the
  path remapping defeated path-based synchronization. That drift is why the
  package was retired: it carried no unique game or design truth while
  requiring hand synchronization that could silently fail.
- Its remaining three mirror-only documents are preserved only in quarantine.
  `START_HERE.md` was reading-order navigation for the package itself.
  `DECISION_GATES.md` recorded the eight closed rollout gates, which
  `docs/HANDOFF.md` still summarizes. `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` recorded Phase
  0-6 traceability, which `docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md` still enumerates. None was
  active direction.
- A checked-in public release ZIP was retired on 2026-08-26 for the same
  reason and is recorded in `AGENTS.md`. The public-repository mirror folder is
  now the only maintained distribution copy.

## Archive Rule

When a new decision supersedes an active one:

1. Update `BALANCE_LEDGER.md` with the new active decision.
2. Add only a concise historical note here when the discarded direction is
   likely to matter later.
3. Do not copy full brainstorming transcripts or every numeric iteration into
   the active ledger.
