Release date: 2026-08-10

## Player-Facing Changes

### Task Choices

- Every separate task-selection window now uses the same card hierarchy as the
  Morning Task Desk.
- Complete-play and future-primary choices show the full task card.
- Printed-effect choices show the effect without unrelated progress or stress.
- Schmoozing Assist choices show only the stats used by the Assist.
- Skill or identity choices show only Skill, rarity, and task name.

### Automation

- Additional Automation Combo cards grant no Skill XP.
- Those cards still advance Combo, Codebase, completion counters, and other
  Complete Play triggers.
- Automation's first milestone states the Skill-XP exception explicitly.
- Combo cards remain in one readable row at supported landscape-phone size.

### Orientation

- The first-play orientation now contains twelve contextual callouts.
- Night guidance moves through Home, Outings, Deals, and Relationships.
- The first Promotion explains path selection and later 6 and 10 XP
  milestones.
- The final callout introduces the first boss floor.
- Advanced card and trait interactions remain discoveries.
