# Workday Long-Cycle Tribrid Discovery

Date: 2026-08-18

## Question

Can the three liked long-cycle strategies create a less tame endurance arc
without changing selected Hybrid D or making the Workday demand constant
attention?

## Protected Material

- Selected Hybrid D production and complete `256-step` Workday composition.
- Candidate C, Passage D, Ensemble C, `112 BPM`, and Hybrid B's motion arc.
- The exact first loop, notes, harmony, roots, form, movement map, and loop
  seam.
- No music is integrated into `officewarsautobattler.html`.

## Focused Comparison

The source-only lab compares two `1024-step`, approximately `4:34.286`
supercycles:

1. **Hybrid D Repeat:** four exact Hybrid D loops.
2. **Tribrid D:**
   - Loop 1: exact Hybrid D baseline.
   - Loop 2: restrained humanized organ phrasing and pocket drift.
   - Loop 3: organ-and-rhythm conversation.
   - Loop 4: the combined conversation plus four lower-register organ
     callbacks, a stronger pocket, and a final pullback before the reset.

The tribrid remains exploratory. Technical verification does not select it.

## Verification

The focused verifier checks:

- exact form, Passage D, Ensemble C, and Hybrid D production preservation;
- exact selected notes across every loop;
- the intended Loop 1-4 variation contracts;
- direct loop starts and live accessible loop highlighting;
- comparison-safe exact first-loop output;
- pause, resume, seek, 48 rapid switches, and stop/replay;
- 100-percent-volume headroom and browser-error capture;
- desktop, compact, and mobile responsive layouts;
- two uninterrupted complete supercycles for both candidates;
- bounded source counts and zero-source cleanup.

The first full attempt stopped before endurance playback because a `1.68`
second loudness sample was too phase-sensitive for two rhythmically identical
first loops. The audio was unchanged. The verifier was corrected to use a
`4.48` second representative window while preserving the same comparison
limit.

The retained build then passed the quick and complete gates. Both candidates
reported:

- `wrapCount: 2`;
- `peakSources: 41`;
- no clipping or browser errors;
- exact reset into Loop 1;
- zero active sources after stop.

At the retained post-wrap snapshot, both candidates reported the same
`signalPeak: 0.099066`.
