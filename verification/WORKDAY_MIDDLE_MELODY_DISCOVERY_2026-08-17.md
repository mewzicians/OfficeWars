# Workday Middle Melody Discovery

Date: 2026-08-17

## Scope

Human listening rejected Band Answers and identified raw middle melodic
material as the earliest unresolved Workday decision. This source-only
discovery lab therefore stops repairing complete forms and compares three
short anonymous journeys before another long-form rebuild.

Every candidate preserves:

- the selected Ensemble C organ-combo relationship;
- the selected `112 BPM` Workday pace;
- a `96-step`, approximately `25.714-second` loop;
- one shared `16-step` departure;
- one shared `16-step` return;
- the same audio graph, transport, monitoring, and output architecture; and
- the melody as protagonist while the organ remains supportive.

Only the `64-step` two-phrase middle identity changes:

1. Candidate A uses a fluid, mostly stepwise continuation.
2. Candidate B uses a more syncopated and playful lead rhythm over Candidate
   A's harmony.
3. Candidate C transforms the contour through a related harmonic detour.

Each candidate provides `Full Journey` and `Melody Only` playback. Candidate
labels were anonymous during discovery.

## Listening Verdict

On 2026-08-18, the user selected Candidate C as the strongest middle.
Candidate C's related harmonic detour is therefore the selected Workday
middle-melody foundation.

This selects Candidate C's exact `64-step` middle melody, its middle harmony,
and its relationship with the selected Ensemble C accompaniment. It does not
approve the complete `96-step` discovery journey as the final Workday cue,
select a long-form structure or duration, finalize arrangement or production,
integrate music into gameplay, or mark any cue shipped. Candidates A and B
remain historical alternatives.
