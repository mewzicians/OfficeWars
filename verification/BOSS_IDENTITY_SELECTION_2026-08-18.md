# Boss Identity Selection

Date: 2026-08-18

## Listening Decision

After comparing all three Full Sketch, Identity Only, and Callbacks Only
versions, the user described Boss A as:

> by far and away the best

The stated reason was that Boss A is the most melodic.

## Selected Scope

Boss A is the selected **boss identity foundation**.

The selection includes:

- its broad rising original hook;
- its galloping bass and broad-backbeat pressure engine;
- its use of On the Clock callbacks for recognition, payoff, and loop
  preparation;
- the `168 BPM` identity-sketch tempo; and
- its current sound and performance relationships as a discovery reference.

Boss B and Boss C remain historical alternatives. Their tactical pressure and
volatile color experiments may inform later supporting details, but neither
identity carries forward as an equal protagonist.

## Open Scope

The selection does not approve:

- the `128-step` sketch as a complete boss form;
- final duration or long-cycle behavior;
- production master or mix translation;
- adaptive states, transitions, victory, defeat, or stingers;
- game integration;
- shipping or final delivery.

## Verification

The audio scheduler was unchanged during selection promotion. The selected
state regression confirms:

- Boss A is the only visually and programmatically selected candidate;
- On the Clock remains the selected parent cue;
- all three hook, pressure-engine, and callback plans remain exact;
- Boss A still passes Full Sketch, Identity Only, and Callbacks Only audio
  checks;
- transport, movement starts, headroom, switching, responsive layout, and
  source cleanup remain valid; and
- no complete boss form or game integration is selected.
